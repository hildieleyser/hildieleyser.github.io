Games are good at driving people into stress reactivity and bad at bringing them out. This one inverts the incentive: the player's own arousal state feeds the game, and progress comes from moving out of fight-or-flight into deliberate decision-making.

Built for the Scientific Game Jam as a playable demonstration of closed-loop neurofeedback.
